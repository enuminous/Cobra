# Limitations and Falsification

COBRA is an experimental behavioral-risk framework.

## Limitations

- Declared objectives may be underspecified.
- Expected behavior may admit many valid actions.
- Proxy divergence may have benign causes.
- Outcome metrics may lag behavior.
- Constraint weights can be subjective.
- Context differences can be legitimate.

## Falsification criteria

COBRA's central hypothesis is weakened if:

1. Standard specification tests perform equivalently.
2. Persistent divergence adds no predictive value.
3. Proxy-goal comparisons do not improve detection.
4. Context-gap analysis adds no value.
5. Results fail on held-out tasks.
6. Risk scores do not correlate with externally verified failures.

## Claim boundary

COBRA measures observable divergence between stated objectives, constraints, behavior, and outcomes. It does not infer hidden intent, deception, motive, or consciousness.
